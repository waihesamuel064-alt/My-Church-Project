About Agape

One church family. One foundation. A love that welcomes everyone.

Agape Community Church is a Christ-centred community in Wakitaka, Jinja, Uganda, committed to loving God, loving people and serving the community.

Our story

From COVID fellowship to a church family.

A fellowship begins

During the COVID period, believers gathered in fellowship, encouraged one another and continued seeking God despite difficult circumstances.

A bigger vision emerges

What began as fellowship became a place for regular worship, biblical teaching, prayer and community.

Agape Community Church

The fellowship developed into a church with a simple heartbeat: God's love should be visible in the way we treat every person.

Core values

What “Agape” means to us.

♡

Love without partiality

We desire to welcome and value people equally because God's love is not reserved for a select few.

⌁

Truth and discipleship

We value Scripture, teaching and spiritual growth that helps believers live out their faith.

♧

Community and service

We believe faith should overflow into practical care, fellowship, outreach and service.

Buyizi branch

Buyizi Agape Community Church

Opened around May 2025, Buyizi Agape Community Church is part of the same growing Agape family.

Buyizi carries the same foundation: Christ at the centre, Scripture as our guide, prayer as our dependence, fellowship as our family life and love as our visible witness.

Its activities include worship, music, fellowship and community engagement. The gallery brings together moments from both Wakitaka and Buyizi.
