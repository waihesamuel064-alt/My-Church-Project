Welcome to Agape

A church built on love, faith and community.

Agape Community Church began as a COVID-era fellowship. What started as a small gathering became a testimony of what God can build when people gather around His Word, pray together and love one another.

Who we are

God meant the fellowship to become a family.

During the disruption of COVID-19, believers found ways to continue encouraging one another and sharing the Word. That fellowship grew beyond a temporary response.

By God's grace, it developed into Agape Community Church — a place where people can worship, learn Scripture, serve, fellowship and experience the love of Christ.

“And above all these put on love, which binds everything together in perfect harmony.” — Colossians 3:14

A growing family

Welcome to Buyizi Agape Community Church.

In around May 2025, a new branch known as Buyizi Agape Community Church was opened.

Buyizi carries the same foundation, faith and values as the Wakitaka church: the love of God, the truth of Scripture, prayer, fellowship, worship and service.

The photographs and activities on this website celebrate the people and ministry happening across both locations.
